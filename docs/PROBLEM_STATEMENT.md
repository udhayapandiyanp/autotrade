# Problem Statement

## 1. Introduction
The used vehicle market is a multi-billion dollar industry characterized by high transaction volumes. However, buying or selling a pre-owned vehicle remains a highly fragmented, stressful, and risky endeavor for individual consumers. Existing general-purpose classified websites or informal social media marketplaces lack the specialized features, security protocols, and trust mechanisms necessary to facilitate smooth, reliable transactions.

## 2. Core Challenges

### A. Lack of Trust and Safety
*   **Information Asymmetry:** Buyers often face undisclosed mechanical defects, falsified vehicle history records, or inaccurate mileage.
*   **Scams and Fraud:** Fake listings, identity theft, and financial scams are common in peer-to-peer marketplaces without structured verification.
*   **No Standardized Reporting:** If a user encounters a fraudulent listing or harassment from another user, there is no efficient, monitored mechanism to report and remove bad actors.

### B. Fragmentation and Inefficient Matching
*   **Dispersed Communication:** Negotiations, inquiries, and schedules are scattered across emails, text messages, and phone calls, making it difficult for sellers to manage multiple offers.
*   **Suboptimal Search and Filtering:** Many existing portals lack robust filters (e.g., mileage, fuel type, transmission, price range, and location), forcing buyers to sift through irrelevant listings.
*   **Tracking Interest:** Sellers do not have a centralized dashboard to track buyer requests or manage inquiries, while buyers struggle to keep track of their favorited listings or sent requests.

### C. Absence of Governance and Quality Control
*   **No Centralized Moderation:** Without an administrator role to monitor listings, platforms become cluttered with spam, duplicate listings, and outdated/expired posts.
*   **No User Account Management:** The lack of structured user profiles (distinct Buyer, Seller, and Admin roles) makes it hard to hold users accountable for bad behavior or to offer customized experiences.

## 3. Impact
These problems lead to financial losses, wasted time, and a general reluctance from potential participants to engage in direct used vehicle trading. The market requires a centralized, transparent, and secure Used Vehicle Trading Platform that bridges the gap between buyers and sellers under the oversight of platform administrators.
